# 79问：第十条诫命是什么？

> 二十六、第十条诫命

**答：第十条诫命是：「不可贪恋人的房屋；也不可贪恋人的妻子、仆婢、牛驴，并他一切所有的」（出20:17；申5:21）。**

Q: Which is the tenth commandment?

A: The tenth commandment is, You shall not covet your neighbor’s house, thou shall not covet your neighbor’s wife, nor his manservant, nor his maidservant, nor his ox, nor his ass, nor any thing that is your neighbor’s.
